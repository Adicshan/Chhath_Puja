Nahay Khay: The first day of purification. Devotees bathe in the river and cook a simple meal of rice, lentils and gourd in clay pots.

Kharna: A day of fasting until evening. Kheer of rice and jaggery is offered to the Sun God before the fast is broken.

Sandhya Arghya: At sunset, devotees stand in the water and offer fruit and prayers to the setting sun.

Usha Arghya: At sunrise, devotees offer prayers to the rising sun, ending the four-day festival.
